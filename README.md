# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end **customer shopping behavior analysis project** that uses Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI to transform transactional data into actionable business insights.

The project analyzes **3,900 customer purchases across multiple product categories** to understand spending patterns, customer segments, product preferences, discount behavior, subscription trends, and revenue contribution.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Understanding customer purchasing behavior is essential for improving customer retention, optimizing promotions, and increasing revenue.

This project follows a complete data analytics workflow:

**Raw Data → Data Cleaning → Feature Engineering → PostgreSQL → SQL Analysis → Power BI Dashboard → Business Recommendations**

The analysis focuses on identifying meaningful patterns in customer transactions and translating them into business-oriented insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze customer spending patterns
* Compare revenue across customer demographics
* Understand subscription behavior
* Identify high-value customer segments
* Analyze the impact of discounts on purchasing behavior
* Identify top-performing and highly rated products
* Compare shipping preferences and purchase values
* Understand revenue contribution across age groups
* Generate actionable business recommendations

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **3,900 rows and 18 columns** covering customer demographics, purchasing information, and shopping behavior.

### Key Features

| Category              | Features                                                       |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Customer Demographics | Age, Gender, Location, Subscription Status                     |
| Purchase Details      | Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount, Season, Size, Color |
| Shopping Behavior     | Discount Applied, Previous Purchases, Frequency of Purchases   |
| Customer Feedback     | Review Rating                                                  |
| Logistics             | Shipping Type                                                  |

The dataset initially contained **37 missing values in the Review Rating column**.

## 🔄 Data Preparation & Cleaning

The data was prepared and cleaned using Python before performing SQL-based business analysis.

### Steps Performed

1. **Data Loading**

   * Imported the dataset using Pandas.

2. **Initial Data Exploration**

   * Used `df.info()` to understand the dataset structure.
   * Used `df.describe()` to examine statistical summaries.

3. **Missing Value Treatment**

   * Identified missing values in the `Review Rating` column.
   * Imputed missing ratings using the **median rating of the respective product category**.

4. **Column Standardization**

   * Renamed columns using `snake_case` naming conventions.

5. **Feature Engineering**

   * Created an `age_group` feature by categorizing customer ages.
   * Created `purchase_frequency_days` from purchase frequency information.

6. **Data Consistency**

   * Checked the relationship between `discount_applied` and `promo_code_used`.
   * Removed the redundant `promo_code_used` column.

7. **Database Integration**

   * Connected Python to PostgreSQL.
   * Loaded the cleaned dataset into PostgreSQL for structured SQL analysis.

## 🔎 SQL Business Analysis

After cleaning the dataset, PostgreSQL was used to answer key business questions.

### Key Analysis Performed

**1. Revenue by Gender**

Compared total revenue generated by male and female customers.

**2. High-Spending Discount Users**

Identified customers who used discounts but still spent more than the average purchase amount.

**3. Top 5 Products by Rating**

Identified products with the highest average customer review ratings.

**4. Shipping Type Analysis**

Compared average purchase amounts between Standard and Express shipping.

**5. Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers**

Compared average spending and total revenue generated by subscribers and non-subscribers.

**6. Discount-Dependent Products**

Identified the five products with the highest percentage of purchases involving discounts.

**7. Customer Segmentation**

Segmented customers into:

* New Customers
* Returning Customers
* Loyal Customers

based on their purchase history.

**8. Top Products by Category**

Identified the top three most-purchased products within each product category.

**9. Repeat Buyers & Subscription Behavior**

Analyzed whether customers with more than five purchases were more likely to subscribe.

**10. Revenue by Age Group**

Calculated the revenue contribution of different customer age groups.

## 💡 Key Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

### 1. Boost Subscriptions

Promote exclusive benefits and incentives to encourage customers to become subscribers.

### 2. Strengthen Customer Loyalty

Introduce loyalty programs and rewards to convert repeat customers into loyal customers.

### 3. Review Discount Strategy

Evaluate discount effectiveness while maintaining control over profit margins.

### 4. Improve Product Positioning

Highlight highly rated and best-selling products in marketing campaigns.

### 5. Target High-Value Customer Groups

Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue age groups and customers associated with higher-value shipping preferences.
